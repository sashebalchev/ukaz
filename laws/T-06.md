# T-06 ALL LAWS HOLD means every check ran and passed

**Law.** `laws check` prints `ALL LAWS HOLD` and exits 0 only if every card above prose grade has a check and each
check exited 0 within its time limit. Otherwise it prints `SOME LAWS FAIL`, names each failing card and exits 1.
When a check ends or is stopped, the tool stops whatever the check left running that it can find, by process
group and by a marker in the environment, and a check that left something running fails.

**Limit.** A process that clears its environment and leaves its process group is not found.

**Why.** A green result has to mean the checks ran. A hung or missing check must not pass quietly, and a check must
not leave processes behind.

**Blocked.** A check that never returns: it is stopped at its time limit with everything it started, and the card
counts as failed. Also blocked: a check that starts a detached process and returns at once: the process is
stopped and the card counts as failed. Also blocked: a card above prose grade with no check.

**Allowed.** A prose-grade card with no check is listed as prose and does not fail the run.
