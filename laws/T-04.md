# T-04 The signing side signs only the list it computed and showed

**Law.** The side that signs for you, the laptop's fingerprint helper or the phone's page, gets the bytes of the
cards from the signing server and computes every hash from those bytes itself. It shows you a list made from
them: id, title and hash of each card. It signs exactly that list, and nothing else. With no list, it signs
nothing.

**Same as.** For the helper this is part of T-09, and for the page and the server it is part of T-11. This card
keeps the one idea over both ways: what you saw is what gets signed. Where it differs from those two, they are
the stricter text.

**Why.** What you see must be what gets signed. The cards come from a laptop where agents run, and the server
only carries them.

**Blocked.** A side shows three cards and signs a list in which one hash is another one. Also blocked: a side
signs when it has no list, for a request that does not exist or was closed. Also blocked: the bytes of a card
change on the server after the list was shown, and the side signs the changed list.

**Allowed.** Three cards wait on the server. The side shows them with the hashes computed from their bytes and
their frozen files, and signs exactly those three lines.
