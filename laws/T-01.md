# T-01 A card's hash covers everything that gives it meaning

**Law.** The hash of a card is computed from the exact bytes of the card file, of every file the card lists as
frozen, and of its figure file if it names one. Changing one byte of any of them changes the hash.

**Why.** An approval has to stop being valid the moment the law, its examples or its check change.

**Blocked.** The test file behind a card's check is edited after approval, and the card still shows as approved.
Also blocked: the picture on a card is redrawn after approval, and the card still shows as approved.

**Allowed.** A file the card does not list changes, and the card stays approved.
