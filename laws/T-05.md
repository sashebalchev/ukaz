# T-05 The gate is open only when nothing is waiting

**Law.** `laws gate` answers open only if every card is approved at its current hash. A new card, a changed card
or a missing signature closes it.

**Why.** The gate is what stops an agent from building on a law you have not seen.

**Blocked.** Ten cards are approved, one is edited, and the gate still answers open.

**Allowed.** Every card is approved and unchanged: the gate answers open.
