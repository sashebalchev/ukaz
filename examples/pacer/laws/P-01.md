# P-01 Kultura opens pages at a calm pace

**Law.** Kultura may open a page at most once every 5 seconds, and at most 40 times in any hour. Whenever both limits allow it, the pacer says yes, so "always no" is not a valid pacer. A "no" uses up nothing. This holds whatever requests arrive, in any order, even if the clock jumps backwards.

**Why.** Facebook and Instagram can block a program that reads their pages too fast. This is the rule for how Kultura treats them.

**Edges.** The hour means "less than 3600 seconds old": a read exactly 3600 seconds old is outside it. Exactly 5 seconds apart is allowed; 4 is not.

**Blocked.** A read 4 seconds after the last one, the 41st read in an hour, and a read when the clock jumped back: refused, and the history stays as it was.

**Allowed.** A first read; a read exactly 5 seconds later; the 40th read of an hour; a read 3600 seconds after the oldest of forty.

**Checked by.** A proof for every request, not a list of tried ones (`laws/P-01/Challenge.lean` is the exact statement; `Paraphrase.md` says it in plain words). The gate also checks 25 example rows, that 13 wrong pacers are refuted, and that the law leaves exactly one behaviour. The proof and the running program are one function.
